Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the Four Labour Codes and do they apply to my business?

India's Four Labour Codes consolidate 29 central labour laws into the Wage Code, Industrial Relations Code, Social Security Code, and Occupational Safety, Health & Working Conditions Code. Applicability depends on your industry, headcount, and state notifications — we assess this specifically for your business.

What is the DPDP Act and why does it matter for my company?

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 governs how businesses collect, store, and process the personal data of individuals in India. Non-compliance can attract significant penalties — we help you build consent frameworks, privacy notices, and data governance policies.

Do startups and SMEs need compliance support?

Yes. Even early-stage companies must meet wage, safety, and data protection obligations. We offer scalable compliance packages suited to growing businesses.

How often should compliance be reviewed?

We recommend a periodic review — at minimum annually, or whenever there is a regulatory change, business expansion, or new hiring policy.
